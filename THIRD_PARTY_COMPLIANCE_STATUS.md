# Beta.3 third-party compliance status

This distribution includes third-party libraries. The license files and THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt are preliminary notices from the project's previous public packaging.

They have NOT been independently verified for all libraries in the new RC3 payload. This archive is not a completed legal compliance report. Review Qt/PySide6 LGPL dynamic-link/relink requirements and corresponding source provisions, plus notices for other packages.

Project QA found GPL-only Qt Virtual Keyboard files excluded and 41 Microsoft JDK-sourced runtime binaries removed from RC3, but final licensing signoff remains open.
